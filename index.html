Fotomop

A simple photo cleaner for iPhone and iPad. Swipe through your library, clear duplicates, screenshots and large videos. Everything runs on your device.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: info@enucuzsistem.com. You can also use Settings → Send Feedback in the app.

FAQ

Are deleted photos gone for good? No. iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted items stay in the Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days.

Do my photos leave my phone? Never. Fotomop has no account, no ads and no analytics.

How do I change the language? Settings → Language in Fotomop, or iOS Settings → Fotomop → Language.
